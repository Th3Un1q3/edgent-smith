# LinkedIn Company Page: Growth-Trends Chart Location

The hiring Growth-trends chart renders ONLY on the company page route `/company/<alias>/home`.
The Posts and About landing views render NO chart.

Verified on the 2026-08-19 German-IT campaign: Zalando, Delivery Hero, and init SE all showed chart-less
Posts/About views; the chart appeared after navigating to the /home route.
