# Audit Consistency: Target Machinery, Not Concept Usage

A skill that audits author-process markers must make its own audit pass truthfully on its own tree (meta-lesson of the skill-quality audit campaign).

- Narrow regexes to MACHINERY — headings (^## Completion Gate) and parenthetical markers ((verify against) — not bare concept usage; document a "concept usage that names a gate to teach it" exception.
- Move reusable multi-line audit scripts to scripts/; one-line greps may stay with the rule that teaches them.
- Exempt mandatory structural negatives from positive-reframe guards: "When Not to Use" sections, routing statements, and the checklist's own pass statements.

Pre-existing flaw: the Rule 14 audit passed while the rule was violated because the grep lacked the exact banned phrase — an audit that cannot see its own violation is provably incomplete. Related: mem:skills/general/building-modular-skills-version-deltas.
