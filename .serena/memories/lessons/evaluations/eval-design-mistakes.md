L0: "Eval design mistakes: how-to prompts, impossible assertions, production pollution, non-existent fixtures, batching before one-case proof, unverified model/agent."
---

- How-to prompts mask usability defects.
- Unobservable or impossible assertions.
- Production memory pollution.
- Non-existent fixtures or wish-list fixtures.
- Batching cases before one-case proof.
- Treating timeouts or ungraded runs as failures.
- Unverified model fit or token cost.
- Orchestrator one-turn assumption.
- Ungraded versus failed confusion.
