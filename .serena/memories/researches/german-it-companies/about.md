# German IT Companies Research

LinkedIn hiring trends research for the 50 German IT companies (47 live / 3 no-data).

## Scope

- LinkedIn company page data (hiring trends, employee counts)
- 12-month growth analysis (Aug 2025 -> Aug 2026)
- Sector-level insights across German IT

## Boundaries (out of scope)

- Non-German companies
- Third-party data sources (Revelio Labs, StockAnalysis)
- Financial performance beyond hiring metrics
