# Researches

Synthesized knowledge artifacts produced by research sessions: curated catalogs, pattern extractions, and topic syntheses, each citing the raw cache entries it derives from via mem: refs.

**Use when:** consuming the conclusions of a research run (video catalogs, pattern extractions, dev-environment syntheses) or checking what a research session already established before re-fetching raw sources.

## Scope

- researches/youtube-ai-engineer-catalog — curated catalog of AI Engineer conference/YouTube videos, synthesized from cached transcripts.
- researches/agentic-patterns-context-memory-patterns — pattern extraction from agentic-patterns.com context/memory pattern pages.
- researches/agentic-patterns-memory-patterns — companion pattern extraction focused on memory patterns.
- researches/connecting-chrome-dev-tools-from-devcontainer — synthesis on connecting Chrome DevTools (protocol/MCP) from inside a devcontainer.
- researches/xml-parsing-skills-loader — synthesis on XML parsing options relevant to the skills loader.

## Boundaries (out of scope)

- Raw fetched content (full transcripts, fetched pages, GitHub issues) lives in cache/; syntheses mem:-reference their sources instead of duplicating them. Cache entries remain the ground truth — syntheses are curated analysis, not raw data.
- Research/context-gathering process knowledge — see mem:research-process/about.
- Sub-agent orchestration and verification of research tasks — see mem:subagent-workflows/about.

## Related Domains

- mem:cache/about — raw source material that researches/ syntheses derive from and cite.
- mem:research-process/about — how research sessions produce these artifacts.
- mem:subagent-workflows/about — how research tasks get dispatched and verified in RUG-style sessions.
- mem:ai-engineering/about — AI engineering people/channels/events feeding researches/youtube-ai-engineer-catalog.
