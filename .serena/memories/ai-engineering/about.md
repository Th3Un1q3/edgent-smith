# ai-engineering

Knowledge domain for AI engineering: key people, YouTube channels, events, and tooling in applied AI engineering.

## Scope
- Curated, verified lists of recent videos from prominent AI engineering YouTube channels (e.g., AI Engineer / @aiDotEngineer).
- References to AI engineering conferences, talks, and events.

## Boundaries (out of scope)
- General machine learning theory without an engineering focus.
- Marketing or product-promotional content.
