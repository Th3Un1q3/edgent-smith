# Reference: Rewrite Rubric and Video-Mapped Examples

Score any 4-component rewrite 0–3 per component (total 12). Ground scores in `cache/youtube-videos/nvc/l7TONauJGfc.txt`.

When to load: you need to grade a Giraffe rewrite or pick a video-anchored before/after.

## Rubric 0–3 Per Component

| Score | Observation | Feeling | Need | Request |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Evaluative label, no facts ("You are rude") | Faux-feeling ("misunderstood, manipulated") | Person-bound strategy ("I need you to…") | Negative demand ("Don't…") or no request |
| 1 | Fact mixed with evaluation ("You yelled") | Vague ("upset, bad") or blended thought | Location/action-bound ("clean sink") | Vague positive ("be nicer") no when/where |
| 2 | Mostly observable, missing time/place/quote | Correct feeling but hedged ("I feel hurt that you…") | Near-universal but still tied to action | Concrete but missing choice or present window |
| 3 | Exact quote + time + place, camera-test passes | Clean body feeling from real list, no judgment inside | Universal, no "you", many strategies possible | Positive, concrete, present, doable + "would you be willing to…?" + accepts no |

Pass: total ≥10/12 and no component scores 0. Fail: any 0 or total ≤9 — revise the lowest component first.
