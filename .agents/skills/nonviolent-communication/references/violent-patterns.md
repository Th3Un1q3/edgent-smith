# Reference: Violent Patterns — 4 Traps From the Video

Diagnose Jackal leaks using video-grounded traps from `cache/youtube-videos/nvc/l7TONauJGfc.txt`.

When to load: you suspect evaluation, faux-feeling, need-as-strategy, or demand and need a table plus probe.

## 1. Evaluation Smuggled as Observation

Present when language adds a story the camera cannot record.

| Trap | Jackal phrase | Camera test failure |
|---|---|---|
| Character label | "You have a big mouth" (teacher) | No quotable words, labels person |
| Uncheckable verb | "You yell at me" (brother) | "Yells" not measurable; use "raised voice" |
| Generalization | "You always embarrass me", "WHO YELLS" (Lincoln High) | No time/place/quote |
| Moral judgment | "You are lazy / disrespectful" | Character verdict, not action |

Probes: What exact words did you hear? When and where? What would a video show?
Repair: Quote words: "When you said '...' yesterday at lunch in the classroom."

## 2. Faux-Feeling (Feeling That Hides a Thought)

Present when "I feel ___" implies what the other did to you.

| Faux (judgment inside) | Hidden thought | Real feeling to use instead |
|---|---|---|
| misunderstood | You don't understand me | hurt, anxious, lonely |
| manipulated | You steered me | angry, scared, tense |
| judged | You evaluated me | hurt, disappointed |
| rejected | You pushed me away | hurt, lonely, scared |
| abandoned | You left me | scared, lonely, anxious |
| betrayed / unheard | You broke trust / didn't listen | disappointed, frustrated, hurt |

Real-feeling inventory (video): angry, scared, disappointed, hurt, anxious, frustrated, lonely, tense, nervous, annoyed, worried.

Probes: Can you say "I feel that I am ___" or "like I was___" after it? If yes, it is faux. What sensation lives in your body?
Repair: "I feel manipulated" → "I feel frustrated and anxious because I think I was not given a choice."

## 3. Need as Strategy (Need Containing "You" or a Location)

Present when a need names a person, place, or action instead of a universal requirement.

| Jackal (strategy) | Why it fails | Giraffe (universal need) |
|---|---|---|
| "I need you to clean the sink" | Names "you" + action | "I need serenity and order when I enter the kitchen" (sink story) |
| "I need the sink clean" | Location-bound strategy | "I need order / consideration" |
| "I need you to listen to me" | Person-bound + vague (11-year fight) | "I need understanding / to be heard" |
| "I need you to stop breaking windows" | Negative strategy (38 windows) | "I need safety / care for our space" |

Probes: Does the sentence contain "you" or a specific action? Would everyone value this? Can many strategies meet it?
Repair: Strip "you" and the action; name a universal noun from [nvc-framework.md](./nvc-framework.md).

## 4. Demand Disguised as Request

Present when "no" triggers punishment or guilt.

| Demand signal | Jackal phrasing | Giraffe repair |
|---|---|---|
| Negative framing | "Don't break windows" (38 windows) | "Would you be willing to play in the backyard?" |
| Vague / not present | "Be more respectful" | "Would you be willing to knock before entering tonight?" |
| No choice | "You have to / must" (Detroit Jackal dialect) | "Would you be willing to…? I accept a no." |
| Punishment for no | Silence, blame if refused | State choice explicitly; plan for no gracefully |

Probes: Does it say what to do (not what to stop)? Is it concrete, present, doable? Is "no" allowed without cost?
Repair: Use the 5 criteria (positive, concrete, present, doable, choice) + "Would you be willing to…?" Keep the action in the next minutes/hours, not "always."
