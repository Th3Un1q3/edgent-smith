# Workflow: Practice Dialogue (Role-Play + Self-Empathy)

Rehearse Giraffe dialogue through self-empathy, empathy guessing, and honest expression.

When to load: user wants to practice NVC, role-play a difficult conversation, or work through self-judgment before speaking.

## Prerequisites

- Load [references/nvc-framework.md](../references/nvc-framework.md) for Giraffe vs Jackal (power-with, big heart, natural giving), [references/violent-patterns.md](../references/violent-patterns.md) for traps to avoid.
- Input: a scenario (who, what happened, what you want to say) and which role to practice.

## Steps

1. **Set the scene** — name the Jackal trigger and your Giraffe intention. Done when: one sentence names the stimulus (camera-test observation) and one sentence names the need you want to serve.
   - Example: Stimulus "Brother left dishes in sink overnight"; intention "connect to make natural giving possible" (Rosenberg, `cache/youtube-videos/nvc/l7TONauJGfc.txt`).

2. **Self-empathy loop** — turn Giraffe inward before outward. Done when: you complete 4 silent sentences to yourself: "When I see/hear [observation], I feel [feeling], I need [need], would I be willing to [self-request]?"
   - Example: "When I see dishes in the sink in the morning, I feel tense because I need serenity. Would I be willing to breathe and remember my need before speaking?"
   - Repeat until judgment softens; do not skip to expression while Jackal chatter runs.

3. **Empathy guess for the other** — imagine their observation, feeling, need in Giraffe. Done when: you voice a guess and check: "Are you feeling… because you need…?"
   - Example: To brother: "Are you feeling tired or overwhelmed because you need rest or ease after a late night?"
   - Video anchor: 11-year listen fight — the need was to be heard, not to win.

4. **Honest expression** — deliver the 4-component message with choice. Done when: you speak one Giraffe sentence and pause for response.
   - Template: "When I [observation], I feel [feeling] because I need [need]. Would you be willing to [request]?"
   - Example sink rewrite: "When I see dishes in the sink in the morning, I feel disappointed because I need order. Would you be willing to rinse your dishes after dinner tonight?"

5. **Role-switch and reflect** — play the other, respond, then debrief. Done when: you note what landed, what triggered Jackal, and one repair.
   - Debrief prompts: Which component stayed Giraffe? Where did evaluation or faux-feeling leak? What one word would make the request more doable? (38 windows lesson: always offer a positive alternative).

## Examples

Dialogue rehearsal:

- You (Giraffe): "When I hear a raised voice in the hallway [Lincoln High anchor], I feel anxious because I need safety. Would you be willing to step outside with me to talk?"
- Partner (Jackal): "You never respect me!"
- You (empathy guess): "Are you feeling angry because you need respect for your space?"
- Partner: "Yes, exactly."
- You (honest expression): "Thank you. Would you be willing to tell me one thing that would help you feel respected right now?"

## Acceptance Criteria

- Scene set with observable trigger and Giraffe intention.
- Self-empathy completed before expression.
- At least one empathy guess voiced with "Are you feeling… because you need…?"
- Honest expression uses the 4-sentence template and choice language.
- Debrief notes one Jackal leak and one concrete repair.
