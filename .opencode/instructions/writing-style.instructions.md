# Communication Style Instructions

Prose mechanics (filler, abstract nouns, passive voice, synonym cycling, punctuation tells) belong to the `unslop` skill at `.agents/skills/unslop/SKILL.md`; load it before drafting and follow it.

## Markdown editing boundary

- A document holds its content, not its biography. Delete claims about its own placement, origin, or design ("this lives here", "the single source of truth" as a process note, "per plan Step N", "we kept X and Y separate") and reading-order notes. A cross-reference that names content elsewhere is content and stays.
- Every sentence tells the reader what to do or know. If it only comments on the document, cut it.
- Base claims on verifiable facts. Label opinion as the writer's perspective, and write "data not available" instead of a vague quantifier.
- Use exact numbers or logically rounded figures. One theme per paragraph, first sentence carries the point.
